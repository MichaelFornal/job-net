Keys

aapprove — goes to Approved_<date>.xlsx
⇧Aapplied — approve and mark it fully applied to, dated today. Skips the /apply checklist entirely: use it right after o when you have just sent the application. u takes both back

mmaybe — revisit later, excluded from the export by default
uundo the last decision
oopen the apply URL in a new tab (does not decide)
xkill this company forever — denies every row it has, now and on every future load. Confirms first; u puts them all back
1-90first ten lanes, numbered in the rail
[]cycle through all the lanes
vcard ↔ grid. The grid shows a lane two dozen rows at a time instead of one, for reading it rather than burning it down. Every key above still means the same thing; decided rows stay on screen struck through rather than vanishing
←↓↑→or hjkl move the grid cursor · ⇧+the same keys, or shift-click, select a range · space picks one cell · Esc clears. With a selection, a/d/m decide every row in it at once and u takes the whole sweep back. o and x always act on the cursor alone — one tab, and one company
dbl-clickopen a job from the grid with the mouse. ⌘-click (ctrl-click on Windows) and middle-click open it too, and leave the cursor where it is — so you can hold a selection, look at three rows in it, and still decide the whole sweep. A plain click still only moves the cursor
gshow/hide the lane rail — every key still works without it
dragthe rail's right edge widens it; double-click that edge fits the longest lane name, or snaps back to the default
tsort — fit / newest posted / oldest posted
/filter the current lane · Esc clear

Fit is the ranking every lane has always used — employer boards before aggregators, proven-US before inferred, strongest AI band first, and for the scored lanes their own score. Newest and Oldest put the posting date in front of that, and leave the rest of it deciding ties. Postings whose source never published a date sort last under both, never first: blank means unknown, not old. The choice sticks across lanes and reloads.

Decisions survive every rebuild. Killed companies are mirrored to triage_blocklist.txt.
